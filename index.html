The House

Built for groups who want to actually spend time together

Open layout and a fully stocked kitchen mean the cooks, the kids, and the post-beach loungers all get their own corner without anyone needing to escape upstairs. Every bedroom is bright with natural light, comfortable bedding, ample storage, and its own TV — with a private balcony for quiet morning coffee.

Outside, a resort-style heated saltwater pool with in-water loungers, a covered patio, and a fire pit make the backyard the real main event. Inside, the game room brings a full-size pool table, two arcade machines, and a big TV, while an upstairs game nook keeps the under-10 crowd happy with board games and beanbags.

Self check-in via smart lock, free driveway + street parking, and beach gear (wagon, cooler, chairs, umbrellas) provided — this one's designed so your group can just show up and start vacationing.

The House

Room for the whole extended family, and then some

Blue Sands sleeps up to 18 across 6 bedrooms, with an elevator running to every level so nobody's stuck hauling bags up the stairs — including a private suite on the ground floor for guests who'd rather skip them entirely.

The backyard centers on a 14x28 saltwater pool with a hot tub, and the game room keeps everyone entertained with arcade games, ping pong, karaoke, and cornhole. It's a quick walk straight to the beach, with fast WiFi (181 Mbps) and a dedicated workspace if someone needs to sneak off and get work done.

Hosted by Brock and Kim — Airbnb Superhosts for 6 years, currently rated 4.99 out of 5 across 74 reviews.
